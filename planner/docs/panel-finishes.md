## Rendering and compatibility

The standard finish colours and material gradients are illustrative. The 63 LC screen swatches were sampled from the centre of the official JUNG front-on product illustrations embedded in https://elektrodesign.pl/les-couleurs-le-corbusier-721. They are **not** calibrated colour specifications or manufacturer RGB values. The UI asks users to confirm a physical sample.

F40, F50 and Ekinex NF use vector finish previews based on their existing reference shapes. The known four-function arrangements provide clickable regions; other binding counts keep the numbered list. LS TOUCH retains its existing functional page editor and changes the frame and screen theme together as an illustrative proposal. Smart Control 5 uses its black reference photo.

The PDF receives the same finish artwork as the editor. User-uploaded reference photos are preserved and are not recoloured. Old finish IDs (including Biały, Czarny, Stal and Mosiądz), unknown custom finishes, assignments, pages, positions and uploaded files are not migrated or removed. Only an explicit new catalogue-model selection applies that model's available palette, as before.
